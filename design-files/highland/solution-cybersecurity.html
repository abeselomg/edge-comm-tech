Cybersecurity

A complete portfolio of network and data security services, plus the staff of security professionals who operate them.

  • NAC
  • IAM
  • MFA
  • SIEM
  • DLP

Security work that starts at the firewall has already skipped the hard part. Edge begins with what the institution actually holds, who needs to reach it, and which of those paths nobody has examined in years, then builds the controls that follow from the answers.

Scope of work

  1. 01 Assessment of the current estate, its exposure and its blind spots
  2. 02 Perimeter design: segmentation, policy and inspection
  3. 03 Identity and access controls across staff, student and supplier accounts
  4. 04 Endpoint protection and patch discipline
  5. 05 Backup and recovery proven by restoring, not by reporting
  6. 06 Policy documentation and staff briefing

What you receive

  • Assessment report with prioritised findings
  • Firewall and segmentation policy
  • Access control matrix
  • A restore procedure that has been tested
  • Incident response runbook

Where we have delivered it

  • Perimeter security and network access control Mizan-Tepi University 2023
  • CCTV, access control and IP telephony Yekatit 12 Hospital 2023

Capabilities

NAC Network access control
A device is checked before it reaches the network, not after it misbehaves.
IAM Identity and access management
One record of who someone is, so leavers actually lose access.
MFA Multi-factor authentication
A stolen password stops being enough, which removes most of the risk.
SIEM Security information and event management
Logs from everywhere in one place, so a pattern is visible rather than buried.
DLP Data loss prevention
Catches records leaving by email or USB before they have gone.
