Who we are

Local expertise connected to global technology

Since 2018, we have helped financial institutions, government organizations, educational institutions, and enterprises modernize infrastructure, secure operations, strengthen resilience, automate services, and create sustainable foundations for digital growth.

We bring together professional expertise, engineering capability, strategic consulting, project management, and technologies from respected global vendors and original equipment manufacturers. This combination allows us to address complex organizational requirements through solutions designed for local operating conditions and aligned with international standards.

From assessment and architecture design to procurement, implementation, integration, testing, training, support, and continuous improvement, we provide one accountable partner throughout the solution lifecycle.

Our journey

From a focused technology company to an integrated solutions partner

Edge Comm-Tech was established to help organizations access, integrate, and sustain world-class technology through dependable local expertise. The milestones below reflect continuous capability development and sector expansion.

  1. March 2018

    Company established in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia.

  2. June 2018

    Delivered the first major technology project and entered the cybersecurity-solutions market.

  3. December 2018

    Completed the first university-sector project.

  4. January 2019

    Entered financial services through the first banking technology project.

  5. February 2019

    Delivered the first smart-classroom project.

  6. January 2020

    Entered datacenter solutions, expanding across facilities, computing, storage, power continuity, and environmental systems.

  7. May 2021

    Introduced power and generator solutions for operational continuity and mission-critical systems.

  8. June 2024

    Expanded into broadcast and satellite solutions.

  9. May 2025

    Expanded into enterprise software, automation, digital platforms, analytics, AI knowledge assistants, private RAG, intelligent voice automation, education AI, local-language AI, and industry-specific agentic AI.

  10. Today

    Operates as an end-to-end systems-integration partner spanning the network edge, core, cloud, intelligent digital platforms, physical infrastructure, and power.
